Analysis

When comparing the waveform and spectrogram to analyse a piece of music I found that waveforms are good for mapping the structure of a piece. This shows pauses and often cues for different sections of a piece (i.e. verse, chorus, refrain etc.) but it lacks enough detail on frequencies. When inspecting the spectrogram I can look at specific frequencies which help to understand the different instruments involved and how closely they are in pitch and loudness. The pieces I have used all feature similar instruments such as horns and drums which will show in similar ways in the spectrogram. However, Celebrity by Charlie Parker and Buddy Rich shows differently as the sound quality is much lower than the others which explains its vast representation.
