Challenges faced when working with music and music-related data


Jazz nusic is often prepared with a lead sheet or arrangement depending on the size of the group of performers. However, many performances and recordings have improvised sections for each of the instruments. this can sometimes make it difficult to find scores with accurate notation and thus edits need to be made when transcribing.
